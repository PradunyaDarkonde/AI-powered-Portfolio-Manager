Help & Support

Find answers to common questions or contact our support team.

Getting started with FinAdvisor is easy! Simply create an account, complete your investment profile, and you'll receive personalized stock recommendations and portfolio insights. You can also connect your existing brokerage accounts to track all your investments in one place.

Our stock recommendations are generated using a combination of fundamental analysis, technical indicators, and machine learning algorithms. We analyze company financials, market trends, and historical performance data to identify investment opportunities that align with your risk profile and financial goals.

Yes, your financial data is secure with FinAdvisor. We use bank-level encryption to protect your personal and financial information. We never sell your data to third parties, and we employ strict security protocols to prevent unauthorized access to your account.

Yes, FinAdvisor supports integration with most major brokerage platforms. You can connect your existing accounts to track all your investments in one place and receive personalized insights based on your current holdings. We use secure API connections to ensure your account credentials are never stored on our servers.

FinAdvisor offers three subscription tiers: Basic, Pro, and Premium. The Basic plan is free and includes limited stock recommendations and portfolio tracking. The Pro plan ($9.99/month) adds advanced analytics, unlimited recommendations, and real-time alerts. The Premium plan ($19.99/month) includes all Pro features plus personalized investment strategies and priority support.
