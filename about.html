About FinAdvisor

Empowering investors with intelligent financial insights since 2015.

Our Mission

At FinAdvisor, our mission is to democratize financial intelligence and empower individuals to make informed investment decisions. We believe that everyone deserves access to sophisticated financial tools and insights, regardless of their investment experience or portfolio size.

We're committed to providing transparent, unbiased investment recommendations and portfolio management tools that help our users achieve their financial goals. By combining cutting-edge technology with financial expertise, we aim to simplify the complex world of investing and make it accessible to all.

Our Story

FinAdvisor was founded in 2015 by Michael Johnson, who recognized a gap in the market for accessible, technology-driven financial advisory services. After years of working in investment banking, Michael saw how advanced financial tools and insights were primarily available to institutional investors and high-net-worth individuals.

With a vision to democratize financial intelligence, Michael assembled a team of finance experts and technology innovators to build a platform that would make sophisticated investment tools accessible to everyone. The company launched with a simple stock recommendation engine and has since evolved into a comprehensive financial advisory platform.

Today, FinAdvisor serves over 500,000 users worldwide, helping them make smarter investment decisions and achieve their financial goals. We continue to innovate and expand our offerings, staying true to our mission of making financial intelligence accessible to all.
